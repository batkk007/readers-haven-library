Library Services

Available facilities, timings, and usage instructions for library visitors.

1. Catalogue

Timings: Open daily, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Online search 24/7)

Instructions: Search books by title, author, or subject in the catalogue and note the call number and section before going to the shelves.

2. E-books

Timings: 24/7 digital access (14-day borrowing period)

Instructions: Log in with your library membership ID on the e-books portal, choose an available title, and read online or download for offline reading.

3. Study-room Booking

Timings: Monday – Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Up to 2 hours per booking)

Instructions: Book a study room at the front desk or online desk at least 1 hour ahead and present your member card on arrival.

4. Printing/Scanning

Timings: Monday – Saturday, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM

Instructions: Insert your USB drive or use the public workstation, select print or scan options, follow fair-use page limits, and collect documents from the desk.

5. Wi-Fi

Timings: Available throughout library opening hours

Instructions: Connect your device to the library Wi-Fi network, open the browser login page, and enter your daily pass or member credentials.
