Library Membership Plans

Choose an annual membership plan to borrow physical books, access study spaces, and enjoy member privileges at Readers' Haven.

Student Plan

PKR 1,500 / year

  • Eligibility: Valid school, college, or university ID
  • Borrowing Limit: Up to 3 books at a time
  • Loan Period: 14 days per book
  • Late-Return Fine: PKR 20 per book / day

Adult Plan

PKR 3,000 / year

  • Eligibility: Residents aged 18 and above
  • Borrowing Limit: Up to 5 books at a time
  • Loan Period: 21 days per book
  • Late-Return Fine: PKR 30 per book / day

Family Plan

PKR 5,500 / year

  • Eligibility: Up to 4 members in the same household
  • Borrowing Limit: Up to 10 books combined
  • Loan Period: 28 days per book
  • Late-Return Fine: PKR 25 per book / day

Borrowing & Late-Return Rules

  • Renewals: Books may be renewed once for an additional 7 days if no other member has reserved the title.
  • Grace Period: A 2-day grace period applies after the due date before daily late fines begin accruing.
  • Account Hold: Borrowing privileges are temporarily paused if overdue fines exceed PKR 500 or a book is overdue by more than 15 days.
  • Lost or Damaged Items: Members must replace the lost/damaged book with an identical edition or pay its current retail replacement cost.
